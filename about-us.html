Est. 12 December 2012

A Welcoming Hotel in Bhilwara Since 2012

Hotel Green Plaza has been welcoming guests since 12 December 2012. Over the years we have grown into one of Bhilwara's most trusted hospitality destinations — known for our clean, comfortable rooms, genuine pure vegetarian food and warm, attentive service.

Positioned conveniently on NH-48 (Bhilwara–Chittorgarh Road), we cater to business travellers, families on holiday, event organisers and highway travellers alike — offering everything needed for a comfortable and convenient stay.
